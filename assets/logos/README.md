# Logos

`mailercloud-logo.png` is the standard mailercloud logo: a navy lowercase wordmark with a blue gradient cloud above the first letters, on a transparent background.

## Official rules (brand guide)

- The name is one word, "mailercloud", written in lowercase with a small "m" and "c" and a cloud icon. The cloud stands for consistent movement, which relates to seamless deliverability.
- The brand guide provides three sets: the standard logo, the favicon (icon-only) version, and a dark-mode version of each. They are in `mailercloud-assets.zip` at mailercloud.com/brand-assets. The dark-mode files are for dark grounds only; the guide shows them on light grounds as the wrong use.

## Use

- Place the standard logo on light and pastel grounds (`bg-paper`, white, `bg-sky`, `bg-lime`, `bg-mint`, `bg-green`, `bg-pink`, `bg-cream`). Use the dark-mode logo on `ink` and `navy` grounds.
- Not yet in this system: the dark-mode logo, the icon-only logo and the official SVG. Until they are added, keep the logo off dark grounds.
- Slides: top-left, `space-margin` (128px) from the left and `space-logo-top` (56px) from the top, 220px wide by 45px high (`object-fit: contain`).
- Reports and proposals: top-left of the header, 24pt high (about 116pt wide), with the title and date right-aligned opposite it.
- Keep clear space of at least the height of the cloud on every side. Never recolour, stretch, outline, add a shadow or rebuild it from type or shapes.
- The file is 1386 × 286 px. Scale it down, not up.
