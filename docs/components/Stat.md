The Stat shows one figure large with a short label, either as a row on a slide or as a KPI tile in a report.

- **Slide row:** figure in `t-title` (64px, bold) or `t-display` (88px) for one hero number; label in `t-body`. Separate stats with a 3px `ink` rule and 40px of padding; no cards.
- **Report tile:** `table-row-alt-bg` fill, `radius-tile` corners, an `r-kpi-label` uppercase label, an `r-kpi` figure and one `r-footnote` line of context ("96.46% of sent, up from 95.89% on 22 Sep").
- **Attention tile:** `attention-bg` fill with an `attention-line` rule down its leading edge, and the label and a text flag in `attention-ink` ("Worth a closer look"). The flag words are required; colour alone is not enough.
- **Report summary box:** `surface-tint` fill, `radius-tile` corners, an `r-subheading` label and `r-body` text. It sits at the top of a report page.
- The figure always carries its unit or a label saying what it counts. Keep digit grouping consistent with the rest of the document.
- The consumer provides the figures. Never invent a statistic; cite the source when it is not MailerCloud's own report.
