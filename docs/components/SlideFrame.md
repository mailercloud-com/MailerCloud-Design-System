The SlideFrame is the fixed chrome around every slide: logo, title, content area and footer, on a full-bleed ground.

**Anatomy (1920 × 1080)**
- Ground: one `bg-*` pastel, never the same as the previous slide.
- Logo: 128px from the left, `space-logo-top` (56px) from the top, 220 × 45px.
- Eyebrow: `space-title-top` (152px) from the top, 24px uppercase with 2px tracking in `text-secondary`: the client name and the section ("Client Name · Design"), so every page says whose it is and where the reader is.
- Title: 8px under the eyebrow, `t-title` (64px), at most 40 characters so it stays on one line. Argument slides use an action title; reference slides may use a noun phrase.
- Content area: 1664px wide, from 32px under the title block down to y 880. The content group is centred in it and fills at most about 65% of it.
- Footer: two parts on one `t-label` row at `space-footer` (64px) from the bottom: `www.mailercloud.com` left, `05 / 18` right. The cover shows only the URL. (Before 3.0 a section label sat between them; it moved into the eyebrow.)

**Rules:** speaker notes on every slide; nothing except the footer below y 880; decorative circles only on the cover and closing slides, off the text column.
